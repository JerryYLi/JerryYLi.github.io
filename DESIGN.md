# Research homepage design

The page helps researchers identify Yi Li's current interests and find papers, projects, code, and the CV. It is a personal academic homepage, with reading and retrieval as its primary tasks.

Preserve the warm white background (#f8f9f6), dark text (#252d2b), restrained teal links (#205b50), existing portrait, factual copy, native expandable bio/interests, and publication selection/search. Secondary text is #58635d; section boundaries use #dce2dc.

Use the original Inter/system sans-serif body stack and Georgia for the name and section headings, as requested by Jerry. Use ordinary section headings, sentence case, a restrained name size, and consistent plain links. Remove decorative labels, colored fragments in the name, repetitive arrows, and borders between every paper. Allow publication content and the real portrait to carry the page's character.

Keep body copy around 60–75 characters per line, larger author text, compact paper spacing, and native keyboard-accessible disclosures. On mobile, research interests stack and links have comfortable tap targets. No animations, external font dependency, marketing CTA, or card grid.

Guidance consulted temporarily: Anthropic frontend-design and Impeccable distill/polish. Skill repositories are in /private/tmp and are not installed into the user's skill directories. The optional Impeccable engine could not download; guidance was applied with source review and browser verification.
